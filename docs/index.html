Inventario PUCMM

Inventario PUCMM was created because the university did not have a platform that stored the history of items borrowed by students. That gap created weak tracking processes and contributed to inventory loss over time.

The project was designed as an internal inventory platform that records lending activity, improves operational visibility, and creates a more reliable history of university assets and student checkouts.

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Supabase, Vercel APIs

open repository
beta product

Resuelvo RD

Resuelvo RD emerged from the need for a more reliable freelance platform in the Dominican Republic, where users could publish their services in a more structured and trustworthy environment.

The platform was designed so users can connect with service providers, communicate directly, review completed work, and leave ratings, helping create more transparency and trust in the local freelance services market.

Node.js, JavaScript, Express, SQL.js / SQLite
